Who We Are

We’re your friendly tech repair team, ready to set up and fix your mobile, tablet or computer, and to help you get the most out of it. You can visit us in store or reach us over the phone.

At Japan Tech we love gadgets and gizmos. We’re the people queuing on launch day for the latest smartphone or gaming console. We’re passionate about tech, but not snobbish about it. We don’t claim to be geniuses, but we are experts at what we do.
